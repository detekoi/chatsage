## Humanizer

- Source: https://github.com/blader/humanizer
- Author: Siqi Chen
- License: MIT

Humanizer is a writing skill that lists the patterns that make text sound AI-written. The De-Slop feature uses a short set of rules adapted from that list. The rules are in `src/lib/deslop.js`.

Humanizer takes its patterns from the Wikipedia page [Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing). WikiProject AI Cleanup maintains that page.
